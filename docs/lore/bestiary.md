## 1. Systemic Integration & Engine Mechanics

To ensure narrative cohesion across procedural roguelike runs, encounters generated by the Narrative Director evaluate three core mechanical layers:

### 1.1 Standard D&D 5e Ability Checks
All skill checks in scenario nodes utilize standard D&D 5e attributes:
* **Strength (STR)**: Physical grappling, spear thrusts, breaking out of suffocating squeezes, and beating heavy brass gongs.
* **Dexterity (DEX)**: Evading ambush strikes, vaulting mangrove roots, dodging fireballs, and outmaneuvering stiff-limbed stalkers.
* **Constitution (CON)**: Resisting bloodborne infections, enduring swamp humidity, and breaking sleep paralysis (`bangungot`).
* **Intelligence (INT)**: Tracking hidden severed torsos, identifying wood origins, deciphering spirit pacts, and analyzing occult lore.
* **Wisdom (WIS)**: Piercing spatial illusions, detecting mimic cries, recognizing circular trails, and resisting hypnotic charm spells.
* **Charisma (CHA)**: Counter-singing sirens, negotiating with tree titans, appeasing demi-goddesses, and performing ritual supplications (`atang`).

### 1.2 ECS Item Condition Flags & Wards
Physical items carried in player inventory maintain dynamic state flags that alter encounter selection probabilities and check difficulties:
* `ItemConditionFlags.Inverted`: Wearing garments inside-out (`baliktad`) disrupts spatial distortion magic (e.g., Tikbalang, Tambaluslos, Tiyanak, Siling). *Mechanic: Disables positive protective stat bonuses on armor while granting +1 WIS against spirit illusions.*
* `ItemConditionFlags.Bloodied`: Weapons or garments soaked in blood attract cadaver-eating ghouls and increase Aswang ambush rates.
* `ItemConditionFlags.Soaked`: Gear saturated by river or sea water reduces movement agility in marshland mud but extinguishes fiery attacks (Santilmo, Bonggo).
* `ItemConditionFlags.Blessed`: Items consecrated through Baliana, Asog, or Babaylan `Atang` rituals grant resistance against dark spellcasting and curses.

### 1.3 Narrative Director Tags
Every bestiary entry defines weighted atmospheric tags used by the probabilistic director to chain encounters based on weather, time of day, and environmental continuity.
